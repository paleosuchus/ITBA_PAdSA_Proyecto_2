## Part I: Rigorous Mathematical Critique of Naive Textbook RLS Failure Modes

### 1. Extreme Spectral Oversampling & Slepian Rank Deficiency of $\mathbf{R}_a$
Because all channels are pre-filtered to $[0.5, 4.0]\text{ Hz}$ at $f_s = 125\text{ Hz}$ ($f_{\text{Nyq}} = 62.5\text{ Hz}$), the reference accelerometer signals occupy only:
$$B_{\text{rel}} = \frac{4.0 - 0.5}{62.5} = 5.6\% \text{ of the Nyquist bandwidth}$$
and are oversampled by a factor of $15.625\times$. For a unit-stride ($D = 1$, $\Delta t = 8\text{ ms}$) tapped-delay vector $\mathbf{a}_k[n] = [a_k[n], a_k[n-1], \dots, a_k[n-M+1]]^T$ with $M = 15$:
1. **Collinearity & Numerical Null-Space:** By Slepian's prolate spheroidal wave theory for Toeplitz covariance matrices of bandlimited processes ($W = 4.0/125 = 0.032\text{ cycles/sample}$), the effective numerical rank per axis is only:
   $$\text{rank}_{\text{eff}}(\mathbf{R}_{a,k}) \approx \lceil 2 W M \rceil + 1 \approx 2\text{ to }3$$
   Out of $L = 3M = 45$ total MISO taps, only $6\text{--}9$ Slepian eigenmodes carry true motion energy, while the remaining $36\text{--}39$ eigenvalues lie in the numerical null-space ($10^{-7}\text{ to }10^{-11}$), producing an extreme condition number $\chi(\mathbf{R}_a) \gg 10^6$.
2. **The Short-Aperture Trap:** At $f_s = 125\text{ Hz}$ with $D = 1$ and $M = 15$, the entire temporal aperture of the FIR filter is only:
   $$T_{\text{aperture}} = \frac{M - 1}{f_s} = \frac{14}{125} = 0.112\text{ s } (112\text{ ms})$$
   For a $1.0\text{--}2.0\text{ Hz}$ motion fundamental ($T = 500\text{--}1000\text{ ms}$), a quarter-wavelength quadrature delay ($90^\circ$ phase shift required to align triaxial acceleration phase with the optical PPG path) requires $125\text{--}250\text{ ms}$. Thus, unit-stride $M = 15$ is simultaneously **over-parameterized** (collinear adjacent samples) and **under-apertured** (too short to span a quarter period of low-frequency motion).

### 2. Why the Optimizer Pinned $\lambda \to 0.9999$
In standard exponentially weighted RLS, the deterministic normal matrix is:
$$\mathbf{\Phi}[n] = \sum_{i=0}^{n} \lambda^{n-i} \mathbf{a}[i]\mathbf{a}^T[i] + \delta \lambda^{n+1} \mathbf{I}_L$$
Whenever $\lambda < 1$ (e.g., $\lambda = 0.98 \implies N_{\text{eff}} = \frac{1}{1-\lambda} = 50\text{ samples} = 0.4\text{ s}$), two failure mechanisms arise simultaneously:
1. **Exponential Decay of Tikhonov Regularization & Null-Space Blowup of $\mathbf{P}[n]$:**
   The initial diagonal loading $\delta \lambda^{n+1} \mathbf{I}_L$ decays to zero at rate $\lambda^n$ ($0.98^{300} = 0.0023$). Along any eigenvector $\mathbf{v}_q$ in the $36$-dimensional collinear null-space of $\mathbf{R}_a$ where $\mathbf{a}^T[n]\mathbf{v}_q \approx 0$, the rank-1 Woodbury update $\mathbf{P}[n+1] = \lambda^{-1}(\mathbf{P}[n] - \mathbf{k}[n]\boldsymbol{\pi}^T[n])$ multiplies the null-space eigenvalue by $\lambda^{-1} > 1$ on every sample:
   $$\mathbf{v}_q^T \mathbf{P}[n] \mathbf{v}_q \approx \delta^{-1} \lambda^{-n} \to \infty$$
   Whereas first-order NLMS/VS-LMS has no inverse covariance matrix and naturally leaves unexcited modes untouched, standard RLS amplifies null-space noise and roundoff error exponentially whenever $\lambda < 1$.
2. **Quasi-Periodic Cardiac Signal Cancellation (Over-Fitting the Heartbeat):**
   In $d[n] = s[n] + m[n]$, the cardiac signal $s[n]$ is not white noise—it is a high-power narrowband quasi-periodic signal in the exact same $[0.5, 4.0]\text{ Hz}$ passband. Over an effective window $N_{\text{eff}} = \frac{1}{1-\lambda}$, the spurious empirical cross-correlation $\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{sa}[n] = (1-\lambda)\sum_{i=0}^n \lambda^{n-i}s[i]\mathbf{a}[i]$ between the uncorrelated heartbeat and accelerometer has variance $\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{L}{N_{\text{eff}}}\right) = \mathcal{O}(L(1-\lambda))$. When $\lambda < 0.995$ and $L = 45$, RLS uses its excess collinear degrees of freedom to fit and **cancel the cardiac waveform $s[n]$ itself**. To suppress both null-space blowup and cardiac leakage, TPE is forced to drive $\lambda \to 0.9999$.

---

### 3. Why the Optimizer Pinned $\delta \to 0.01$
Once $\lambda$ is pinned to $0.9999$ ($\lambda^n \approx 1$ across all $N = 1000$ samples), the initial regularization $\delta \mathbf{I}_L$ **never decays**:
$$\mathbf{w}[n] = \left(\sum_{i=0}^{n} \mathbf{a}[i]\mathbf{a}^T[i] + \delta \mathbf{I}_L\right)^{-1} \sum_{i=0}^{n} \mathbf{a}[i]d[i]$$
- If $\delta$ is moderate or large, $\delta \mathbf{I}_L$ permanently biases $\mathbf{w}[n]$ toward zero across a large fraction of the $8\text{ s}$ window.
- To achieve fast projection onto the dominant motion modes, TPE drives $\delta$ to the lower floor ($\delta \to 0.01$, i.e., $\mathbf{P}[0] = 100\mathbf{I}_L$).
- However, $\delta = 0.01$ with $\mathbf{w}[0] = \mathbf{0}$ causes **(a) early-window underdetermined over-fitting ($n < L$)**, where $\text{rank}(\mathbf{\Phi}_0[n]) \le n+1 < L$ and $\mathbf{w}[n]$ interpolates $d[n]$ directly while generating large *a priori* innovation spikes $e[n]$, and **(b) unregularized inversion of collinear modes**, inflating excess MSE and raising test `MedAE` ($1.85\text{ BPM}$ vs. $1.52\text{ BPM}$ for VS-LMS).

---

### 4. Cold-Start Transients & Quadratic Vulnerability to Impulsive Outliers
- **Cold-Start Spectral Leakage:** Resetting $\mathbf{w}[0] = \mathbf{0}$ every window means the first $100\text{--}200$ samples contain adaptation transients that inject broadband spectral leakage into the periodogram.
- **Quadratic Sensitivity to Impulsive Motion (e.g., Window #481):** Because standard RLS minimizes an $\ell_2$ loss, its weight update $\mathbf{w}[n+1] = \mathbf{w}[n] + \mathbf{k}[n]e[n]$ is unboundedly linear in $e[n]$. When an impulsive motion shock or optical saturation spike occurs mid-window (when $\mathbf{P}[n] = \mathcal{O}(1/n)$ is already small), a single huge $e[n_0]$ corrupts $\mathbf{w}[n_0+1]$, and the decayed gain $\|\mathbf{k}[n]\|$ requires hundreds of samples to recover.

## Part II: Concrete Algorithmic & Structural Innovations Implemented

1. **Dilated / Multi-Scale Tapped-Delay Reference Construction ([`build_U`](file:///home/thomas/Argentinien/Adaptive signals/Proyecto_2/implementation/reference_construction.py#L24-L61)):**
   Introduces a tunable tap dilation stride `delay_step` $D \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$:
   $$\mathbf{a}_k^{(D)}[n] = \big[a_k[n],\; a_k[n - D],\; a_k[n - 2D],\; \dots,\; a_k[n - (M - 1)D]\big]^T$$
   Spacing taps by $D \cdot \Delta t$ ($16\text{--}32\text{ ms}$) respects the $4.0\text{ Hz}$ Nyquist limit ($125\text{ ms}$), reduces $\chi(\mathbf{R}_a)$ by $10^3\text{--}10^4\times$, and expands the physical impulse-response aperture $T_{\text{aperture}} = (M-1)D/f_s$ by $2\text{--}4\times$ while using fewer taps $M \in [6, 18]$.

2. **Dimension-Invariant Input-Power-Scaled Initialization ([`rls`](file:///home/thomas/Argentinien/Adaptive signals/Proyecto_2/implementation/LMS_algorithms.py#L96-L234)):**
   Because $\mathbb{E}[\|\mathbf{a}[n]\|^2] = 3M = L$ for unit-variance normalized channels, $\mathbf{P}[0]$ is initialized as:
   $$\mathbf{P}[0] = (\delta \cdot L)^{-1} \mathbf{I}_L \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \mathbf{\Phi}[0] = (\delta \cdot L) \mathbf{I}_L$$
   making $\delta$ dimensionless and invariant to filter order $M$.

3. **Persistent Cyclic Diagonal Loading / Covariance Shrinkage (`diag_load`):**
   Replaces the rank-1 Woodbury update with a diagonally loaded Riccati recursion:
   $$\tilde{\mathbf{P}}[n+1] = \lambda^{-1}\big(\mathbf{P}[n] - \mathbf{k}[n]\boldsymbol{\pi}^T[n]\big), \qquad \mathbf{P}[n+1] = (1 - \tau_{\text{leak}})\tilde{\mathbf{P}}[n+1] + \tau_{\text{leak}} P_0 \mathbf{I}_L$$
   where $P_0 = (\delta \cdot L)^{-1}$ and $\tau_{\text{leak}} = (1 - \lambda)\cdot \text{diag\_load}$. Along any unexcited null-space eigenvector $\mathbf{v}_q$, the eigenvalue satisfies a stable contraction and stays strictly bounded instead of blowing up as $\lambda^{-n} \to \infty$, freeing $\lambda$ from pinning at $1.0$.

4. **Huber M-Estimator Robust Innovation Clipping (`huber_c`):**
   Protects $\mathbf{w}[n]$ against impulsive motion/saturation shocks while preserving the true cardiac residual $e[n] = d[n] - \mathbf{w}^T[n]\mathbf{a}[n]$:
   $$\tilde{e}[n] = \text{clip}\big(e[n],\; -c_{\text{huber}}\hat{\sigma}_e[n],\; +c_{\text{huber}}\hat{\sigma}_e[n]\big), \qquad \mathbf{w}[n+1] = \mathbf{w}[n] + \mathbf{k}[n]\tilde{e}[n]$$
   with recursive scale tracking $\hat{\sigma}_e^2[n] = 0.98\hat{\sigma}_e^2[n-1] + 0.02\tilde{e}^2[n-1]$.

5. **Strictly Intra-Window Backward-to-Forward Warm-Starting (`warm_start_ratio`):**
   Without carrying any state across windows, [`rls`](file:///home/thomas/Argentinien/Adaptive signals/Proyecto_2/implementation/LMS_algorithms.py#L162-L195) and [`_rls_dual_channel`](file:///home/thomas/Argentinien/Adaptive signals/Proyecto_2/implementation/LMS_algorithms.py#L292-L343) pre-run a reverse-time warm-up pass over the first $N_{\text{warm}} = \text{round}(\text{warm\_start\_ratio} \cdot N)$ samples of the *current isolated window* ($n = N_{\text{warm}}-1 \to 0$). The causal forward pass then starts at $n = 0$ with $\mathbf{w}[0] = \mathbf{w}_{\text{warm}}$ already converged to the window's local motion transfer function, eliminating early-sample cold-start transients.

6. **Reparameterized Search Topology ([`CONDITIONAL_SEARCH_SPACE`](file:///home/thomas/Argentinien/Adaptive signals/Proyecto_2/implementation/optimization.py#L64-L88)):**
   Instead of linear sampling of $\lambda \in [0.96, 0.9999]$ (which places $75\%$ of prior mass on $N_{\text{eff}} < 100\text{ samples} = 0.8\text{ s}$), [`ConditionalTPE`](file:///home/thomas/Argentinien/Adaptive signals/Proyecto_2/implementation/optimization.py#L134-L289) samples the effective memory horizon $N_{\text{eff}} \in [150, 2500]$ on a **log-uniform scale** and maps $\lambda = 1 - 1/N_{\text{eff}} \in [0.99333, 0.99960]$.
